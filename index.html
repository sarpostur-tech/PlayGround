How to play

Take aim: Adjust angle and power, or drag from your worm to aim. The dotted guide previews your shot.

Build: Choose a wall, floor, or ramp, then tap BUILD and place it in the arena. Build a little cover before firing.

Fire: Choose shell or bouncer. Hits damage the rival and blasts terrain. You have 100 wood to start — spend it wisely!

The rival takes a shot after your turn. First worm to 0 health loses.
